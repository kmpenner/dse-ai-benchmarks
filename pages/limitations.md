# Limitations

(From §2.5 of the paper.)

- The benchmark measures single responses to single tasks, not the time a human needs to correct a draft.
- The judges are language models reading text. They do not see the manuscript image.
- The Greek and Latin texts have published critical editions, which a model could recite from memory. Neither edition is diplomatic, so recitation is penalized by the Greek and strict Latin scoring. The loose Latin CER is less able to tell reading from recall.
