# Leningrad Codex, folio 220B

Selected models transcribed the page image into TEI P5. With no published diplomatic transcription, we scored each output against the consensus (medoid) of the others, separately for the consonants and the masorah.

- Transcriptions: [`resac2026/leningrad_F220B_transcriptions/`](https://github.com/kmpenner/dse-ai-benchmarks/tree/master/resac2026/leningrad_F220B_transcriptions)
- Scores: [consensus_scores.csv](resac2026/leningrad_F220B_transcriptions/consensus_scores.csv) · [summary.csv](resac2026/leningrad_F220B_transcriptions/summary.csv)

Consensus measures agreement between models, not accuracy. On the biblical text, models that agree may simply be reproducing the standard edition, so the scores for the marginalia are the more informative.
